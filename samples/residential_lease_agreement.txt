RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of the Effective Date by and between Maple Ridge Properties LLC ("Landlord") and John Doe ("Tenant").

SECTION 1: PREMISES AND TERM
1.1 Premises: Landlord hereby leases to Tenant and Tenant leases from Landlord the residential premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Apartment 4B, Springfield ("Premises").
1.2 Term: The term of this Lease shall commence on October 1st and continue for a period of twelve (12) full calendar months.
1.3 Automatic Renewal: UNLESS TENANT PROVIDES WRITTEN NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL AT LEAST SIXTY (60) DAYS PRIOR TO THE EXPIRATION OF THE INITIAL TERM, THIS LEASE SHALL AUTOMATICALLY RENEW FOR AN ADDITIONAL TWELVE (12) MONTH TERM UNDER THE SAME CONDITIONS, EXCEPT THAT RENT SHALL AUTOMATICALLY INCREASE BY TEN PERCENT (10%). Failure to provide written notice via certified mail strictly within this window forfeits Tenant's right to vacate.

SECTION 2: RENT AND PAYMENT
2.1 Monthly Rent: Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly rent in the amount of $2,200.00, payable in advance on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month.
2.2 Late Fees: If rent is not received by Landlord by 11:59 PM on the third (3rd) calendar day of the month, a late fee of $150.00 shall immediately apply, plus an additional daily penalty of $25.00 per day until paid in full.
2.3 Payment Method: All payments must be made exclusively through Landlord's designated electronic portal. Tenant is responsible for all third-party processing transaction fees ($35.00 per transaction).

SECTION 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 Deposit Amount: Upon signing, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $4,400.00 (two months' rent) as a security deposit for faithful performance of Tenant's obligations.
3.2 Deductions and Forfeiture: Landlord may retain all or part of the security deposit for unpaid rent, late fees, repairs, repainting, deep cleaning, or administrative processing fees. Landlord shall have ninety (90) days following surrender of possession to deliver an itemized statement and any remaining balance. If Tenant terminates early for any reason, the entire deposit is automatically forfeited as liquidated damages.

SECTION 4: TERMINATION AND EARLY CANCELLATION
4.1 Early Termination: Tenant possesses no right to early termination of this Lease. In the event Tenant vacates, abandons, or requests early release, Tenant remains strictly liable for all rent payments due through the remainder of the full lease term, plus an Early Break Fee equivalent to two (2) months' rent ($4,400.00).
4.2 Landlord's Immediate Right of Termination: Landlord may terminate this Lease immediately upon three (3) days' notice if Tenant violates any community rule or engages in any objectionable conduct as determined in Landlord's sole discretion.

SECTION 5: MAINTENANCE, REPAIRS, AND ENTRY
5.1 Maintenance: Tenant shall maintain the Premises in clean, sanitary condition. Tenant is strictly liable for any plumbing stoppage, window breakage, or appliance repair cost under $300.00, regardless of cause or prior wear-and-tear.
5.2 Landlord Access: Landlord and its contractors reserve the right to enter the Premises at any time without prior notice for emergencies, or upon four (4) hours' verbal or electronic notice for inspections, appraisals, routine maintenance, or showings to prospective tenants or buyers.

SECTION 6: RESTRICTIONS AND CONDUCT
6.1 Subletting and Guests: Subletting, assignment, Airbnb/short-term rentals, or overnight guests staying more than three (3) consecutive nights without Landlord's prior written consent is strictly prohibited and constitutes an incurable breach.
6.2 Pets: No pets, emotional support animals, or visitors with animals are allowed on the Premises without written approval and a non-refundable $750 pet admission fee plus $75 monthly pet rent.

SECTION 7: INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY WAIVER
7.1 Limitation of Liability: Landlord and its agents shall not be liable for any damage, loss, or injury to Tenant, Tenant's family, or guests, or to Tenant's personal property resulting from fire, water, burst pipes, theft, mold, structural defects, HVAC failure, or vandalism, even if caused by the ordinary negligence of Landlord. Tenant waives all rights of subrogation.
7.2 Tenant Indemnity: Tenant agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Landlord from any and all claims, lawsuits, costs, and attorney's fees arising from Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises.

SECTION 8: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW
8.1 Mandatory Binding Arbitration: Any controversy, claim, or dispute arising out of or relating to this Lease shall be settled by binding arbitration before a single arbitrator selected solely by Landlord. Tenant explicitly waives any right to a trial by jury.
8.2 Class Action Waiver: Tenant agrees that all claims must be brought on an individual basis only, and Tenant explicitly waives any right to participate as a plaintiff or class member in any class, collective, or representative proceeding.
8.3 Attorney Fees: In the event of any legal dispute, Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for all legal and attorney fees incurred by Landlord, regardless of whether Landlord prevails in the dispute.
